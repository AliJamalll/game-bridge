Game Bridge

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 18, 2026

What the App collects

Game Bridge does not collect analytics, and it never sees your Steam username, password or games. Your Steam sign-in happens inside the Steam client, directly with Valve.

Free trial

So the free trial can't be restarted by reinstalling, the App sends our server a one-way hash (SHA-256) of your Mac's hardware ID, together with the date your trial started. The hash can't be turned back into the hardware ID and isn't linked to your name, email or Apple ID. We keep only the hash and the trial start date. Your IP address is seen by our hosting provider (Vercel) as part of the request.

Founders offer

While the founders offer is open, the App registers your Mac for a free-for-life seat using the same one-way hash of your Mac's hardware ID used for the free trial. We keep only that hash, to count seats and to confirm yours.

Game ratings

After you start a game from Game Bridge, it may ask how the game ran. If you answer, the App sends the game's Steam ID and name, your answer, and the same one-way hash of your Mac's hardware ID used for the free trial (so each Mac counts once). The totals are shown publicly on our website. Nothing is sent if you skip the question.

Updates

The App checks game-bridge.vercel.app once a day for a newer version, and downloads it from GitHub when you choose to install it. The check sends standard request information such as your IP address and the App and macOS versions.

License checks

When you activate a license, the App sends your license key and your Mac's name to Polar's license service (polar.sh) to verify it. The App re-checks the license periodically.

Downloads

During setup the App downloads Wine from GitHub and the Steam installer from Valve. Those services receive standard request information such as your IP address.

Purchases

Orders are handled by Polar as merchant of record. We receive your email address and order details to provide support; we do not receive your full card details. See Polar's privacy policy for how they process payment data.
