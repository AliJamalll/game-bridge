Game Bridge

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 18, 2026

What the App collects

Game Bridge does not collect analytics, and it never sees your Steam username, password or games. Your Steam sign-in happens inside the Steam client, directly with Valve.

License checks

When you activate a license, the App sends your license key and your Mac's name to Lemon Squeezy's license service to verify it. The App re-checks the license periodically.

Downloads

During setup the App downloads Wine from GitHub and the Steam installer from Valve. Those services receive standard request information such as your IP address.

Purchases

Orders are handled by Lemon Squeezy as merchant of record. We receive your email address and order details to provide support; we do not receive your full card details. See Lemon Squeezy's privacy policy for how they process payment data.
