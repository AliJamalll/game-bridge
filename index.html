How it works

1. One-click setup

Game Bridge downloads a compatibility layer (Wine) and the official Steam installer directly from Valve, and configures everything for you.

2. Launch Steam

Press “Launch Steam”. Steam opens in its own window, ready to sign in to your account and browse your library.

3. Stays working

When Steam updates itself, Game Bridge automatically re-applies the fixes that keep Steam's interface from turning into a black window on Mac.

FAQ

Do I need to buy my games again?

No. You sign in to your own Steam account and use the games you already own.

Will every Windows game work?

Not every game. Many games run well, but titles that use anti-cheat or very new graphics features may not start. Try them during the free trial before subscribing.

What does the trial include?

Everything. After 10 days, choose a monthly or yearly subscription to keep using Game Bridge.

Is Game Bridge made by Valve?

No. Game Bridge is an independent app and is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Valve Corporation. Steam is downloaded directly from Valve's servers.

How do I cancel?

Use the link in your purchase receipt email to manage or cancel your subscription at any time.
